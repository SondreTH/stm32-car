Team 7 Car Wiring

What to solder where on the perfboard that sits on the NUCLEO-F446RE's inner morpho rows. It matches the pins in Team7_Car.ioc (CubeIDE) and config.h (Arduino).

VBAT 11–12.6 V 6 V servo 5 V 3.3 V GND Signal Motor current

Connector rule

Male pins on the perfboard

Every header J1–J6 on the perfboard is a male pin header. Cables end in female plugs. Your motor/encoder and servo cables already work this way.

Modules: female–female jumpers

The Bluetooth, MPU-6050, HC-SR04 and L298N logic pins are male pins on the module, so connect them with female–female Dupont wires (a 4-wire ribbon keeps them together).

Power: screw terminals

The 5 V and 6 V inputs (J7, J8) and the battery wiring use screw terminals or soldered wire. Dupont plugs can wiggle loose and don't like amps.

Label every plug

J4, J5 and J6 are all 4-pin. Mark each header and its cable with the same colour dot. Swapping them puts 5 V on a signal pin.

Power

The battery feeds a terminal block (for example WAGO 221). From there each part gets its own supply wire. Only 5 V, 6 V and the battery-sense wire go to the perfboard.

Solder a 1000 µF 16 V capacitor across J2 pins 1–2 (servo), and 100 µF across the 5 V rail. Set both bucks with a multimeter before anything is plugged into them.

Nucleo pins under the perfboard

Top view, USB end at the top. Your female headers sit on the inner row of each side. Coloured dots are the pins to wire. Row 1 and row 19 are unused, so either end can be cut off.

Leave CN7-18 (+5V) unconnected. With the power jumper on E5V it is the same 5 V anyway, and wiring both invites two supplies fighting when someone moves the jumper.

Perfboard headers

Pin 1 of each header is listed first. The pin order matches the device or cable, so a straight 4-wire ribbon or the existing plug fits without crossing wires. Check the motor cable colours against your motor's label before soldering J1.

Before the first power-up

  1. Continuity: the GND rail beeps to CN7-20, CN7-22 and CN10-9.
  2. No shorts: 5V–GND, 3V3–GND, 6V–GND and VBAT–GND must not beep.
  3. Power the bucks with nothing attached. Set 6.0 V (XL4015) and 5.0 V.
  4. Power jumper JP5 on E5V. Switch the battery on first, then plug in USB.
  5. Measure PA7 (CN10-15) while holding a hand 10 cm in front of the HC-SR04: it must never go above 3.3 V.
  6. Only then plug the servo, motor and modules into their headers.
The L298N's 5V screw pin is an output from its own regulator. Connecting it to your 5 V rail makes two supplies fight. Leave it empty.
